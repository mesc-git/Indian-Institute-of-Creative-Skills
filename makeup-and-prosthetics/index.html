About Course

The Makeup & Prosthetics Certificate Program at the Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS) is a professionally designed, skill-focused program that offers flexible learning paths for students interested in beauty makeup as well as advanced prosthetic and character makeup.

Students receive hands-on training through practical sessions, live demonstrations, and portfolio-based learning, preparing them for careers in beauty, fashion, media, film, theatre, and live entertainment.

Course Highlights

Industry-oriented makeup & prosthetics curriculum

Flexible duration options based on career goals

Hands-on practical training with professional tools

Focus on beauty, character, and transformation makeup

Certification from Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS)

Tools & Technologies You Will Learn

Makeup Tools & Practices

  • Professional makeup brushes and kits
  • Skin preparation and base application
  • Beauty, fashion, and character makeup techniques
  • Hygiene and safety standards

Prosthetics Techniques (6-Month Option Only)

  • Prosthetic materials, adhesives & safety

Career Outcomes

  • ✔ Perform professional makeup applications

  • ✔Apply prosthetics for character and visual transformation (6-month option)
  • ✔ Maintain professional hygiene and set etiquette
  • ✔ Build a strong makeup or makeup & prosthetics portfolio

Certification

Students who successfully complete the program will receive a Makeup & Prosthetics Certificate from the Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS) , in association with the Media & Entertainment Skills Council (MESC) and the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) .
