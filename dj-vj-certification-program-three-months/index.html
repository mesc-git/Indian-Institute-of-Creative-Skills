About Course

The Certification Course in DJ at the Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS) is a professionally designed course that trains students in the art of DJing and live visual performance. This program focuses on music mixing, live performance techniques, and audio-visual synchronization required for clubs, events, concerts, festivals, and digital platforms.
Students gain hands-on exposure to professional DJ equipment, performance workflows, and VJ tools, enabling them to confidently perform and manage live shows in the entertainment industry.

Meet Your Mentor

Navdeep Ahuja

Founder, Patsav Academy · Industry Mentor · Live Performance Expert

Navdeep Ahuja is a highly respected name in India’s entertainment and creative education ecosystem, known for his extensive contribution to DJing, live performance, and skill-based training.

With over two decades of hands-on industry experience, he has played a pivotal role in shaping careers across DJing, VJing, sound engineering, and live event production.

Widely recognized in the industry as VDJ NDA, he is the founder and visionary force behind Patsav Academy, one of India’s most established institutes for DJing, electronic music, visual performance, and sound engineering education.

As a mentor, Navdeep emphasizes practical exposure, performance confidence, and industry-ready skills. His teaching philosophy focuses on creativity, discipline, and professional ethics—ensuring students are prepared for long-term careers in the entertainment industry.
