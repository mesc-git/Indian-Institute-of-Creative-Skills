Program Vision

The IGNITE program at IICS blends graphic design foundations with interactive, motion, and spatial design to create industry-ready experience designers.

Integrated Design Foundations

Builds strong graphic design fundamentals while expanding into interaction, motion, UI/UX, and spatial design.

Experience-Driven Learning

Focuses on designing meaningful experiences across media, technology, events, retail, and immersive platforms.

Motion, Interactive & Spatial Practice

Trains learners to work with dynamic visuals, interfaces, environments, and real-time design systems.

Industry-Ready Design Thinkers

Prepares graduates to design for screens, interfaces, and physical-digital spaces beyond static visual design.

Pedagogy & Learning Model

A studio-driven, experience-focused approach designed to develop real-world, industry-ready designers.

Studio-Based Learning
  • 01

    Studio-based visual and interaction design training

  • 02

    Iterative design projects and critique-driven learning

  • 03

    Live design briefs and simulated client-based workflows

  • 04

    Regular studio juries and structured feedback sessions

  • 05

    Portfolio-based evaluation system focused on execution

  • 06

    Strong emphasis on design thinking and real-world application
