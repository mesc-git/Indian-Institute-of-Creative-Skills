About Indian Institute of Creative Skills

Indian Institute of Creative Skills operating under the aegis Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Government of India, is India's First Creative Incubator. It is designed to establish India as a global Creative powerhouse by empowering the next gen creative entrepreneurs, operated by the Media and Entertainment Skills Council & Funded by NSDC.

How
to Apply

Step 1

Fill out the application form for MECAT IICS and submit it.

Step 2

After submission, you’ll receive your login credentials at the email address you provided.

Step 3

Click the Login button and sign in using your username and password.

Step 4

Once logged in, you’ll be redirected to your dashboard — complete your application form there.

Step 5

Pay the application fee for MECAT IICS and submit your final application.
