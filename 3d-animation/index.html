Course Curriculum

About Course

The 3D Animation for Media & Entertainment program is designed to prepare students for real production environments in animation, VFX, and digital content creation. Through a studio-style curriculum, students learn 3D modeling, character animation, lighting, compositing, and complete an apprenticeship that provides hands-on production exposure.

  • Realistic motion principles
  • Posing & body mechanics
  • Creating functional rigs

  • Advanced lighting techniques
  • Arnold / V-Ray workflows
  • Film-grade rendering

  • Integrating 3D with live-action
  • Nuke / After Effects compositing
  • Camera tracking & matchmoving

  • Character & creature modeling
  • Environment & prop modeling
  • Topology & detailing techniques

Software You Will Learn

  • Autodesk Maya
  • Blender
  • ZBrush
  • Substance Painter
  • Arnold / V-Ray
  • Nuke
  • Adobe After Effects
  • Adobe Photoshop
