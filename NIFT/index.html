NIFT Entrance Exam 2026: Registration (Extended till Jan 13), Exam Date (OUT), Complete Guide for Fashion & Costume Design Aspirants

Everything you need to know about preparing for India's premier fashion institute entrance exam

The National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) entrance examination is one of the most competitive exams for aspiring fashion designers in India. Each year, thousands of students compete for limited seats in NIFT's prestigious programs.

This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about the NIFT entrance exam, from understanding the pattern to exploring alternative pathways in fashion and costume design education.

Understanding NIFT

Key Facts About NIFT

  • . Established in 1986 under Ministry of Textiles
  • . 18 campuses across India
  • . Offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs
  • . Considered India's premier fashion institute

NIFT Latest Notification

  • IFTEE 2026 applications are available on the official NIFT and NTA NIFT websites.
  • Registration period: 8 December 2025 to 13 January 2026.
  • Application fee: ₹500 for SC / ST / PwD candidates, ₹2,000 for General / OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) / GEN-EWS candidates
  • Eligibility criteria: M.Des: Graduation from a recognized university, B.Des: Passed Class 12 and maximum age limit of 24 years
  • Interested candidates must fill out the online application form and pay the registration fee to complete the process.

Exam Pattern & Syllabus

Written Test Components

Creative Ability Test (CAT)

  • Duration: 3 hours
  • Tests creative thinking and design skills
  • Visual perception and composition
  • Color theory application

General Ability Test (GAT)

  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Quantitative ability
  • Communication ability

NIFT 2026 - Exam Preparation Tips

The second week of February 2026 is when the NIFT 2026 written exams are anticipated to take place. Examinees should be aware that the CAT paper has the most weight. Therefore, candidates must properly prepare for every topic listed in the exam syllabus.

NIFT General Ability Test (GAT) Preparation Tips

A few pointers for NIFT GAT include:

  • Examine the syllabus and exam format carefully. Include any changes to the curriculum or pattern in your preparation.
  • For NIFT 2026, make sure you are reading the best books.
  • Create a practical study plan and follow it. Get ready for NIFT 2026 as soon as possible. This will guarantee that you have adequate time to finish the syllabus, work through sample papers, take practice exams and revise.
  • While studying, make notes. If the notes are adequate, you can quickly review them rather than reading lengthy passages of text. This can result in significant time savings that can be put to better use.
  • Solve as many sample papers as you can. Additionally, complete the NIFT question papers from previous years.
  • Practise mock exams throughout the final week to assess your readiness.
  • Maintaining your health and optimism is also crucial.

Creative Ability Test (CAT) Preparation Tips

To get prepared for the 2026 NIFT CAT:

  • Develop your ability to observe and be innovative.
  • Learn the nuances of geometrical shapes.
  • Learn to conceptualise before you draw anything.
  • To score well on the CAT, jittery and unclear lines are a big no-no. So, get better at drawing lines.
  • For CAT, shading is not a crucial ability, but mastering the fundamentals is.
  • Learn how to draw in a variety of styles, including human, figure, cartoon, perspective, geometric, and doodle.
