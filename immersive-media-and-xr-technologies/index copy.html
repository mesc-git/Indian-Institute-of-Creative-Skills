Program Overview

The B.Sc in Immersive Media & Digital Production Design is a next-generation degree designed for students who want to shape how humans interact with digital worlds.

This program blends design thinking, immersive storytelling, real-time engines, AI-driven workflows, and experiential technologies to create meaningful Digital Production across entertainment, education, enterprise, healthcare, marketing, and emerging metaverse ecosystems.

Unlike traditional media programs, this degree focuses on experience creation, not just content production.
