About Course

The AI Photography & Digital Reels Certificate Program at the Indian Institute of Creative Skills (IICS) is a future-ready course designed to train students in modern photography, AI-powered image creation, and high-impact short-form video content for social media and digital platforms. This program focuses on visual storytelling, AI-based image generation, photo editing, reel creation, transitions, effects, and platform-specific content strategies for Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and other digital channels.
Students gain hands-on experience with AI tools, cameras, smartphones, and editing software to create professional-quality photos and viral digital reels for brands, creators, and personal portfolios.
