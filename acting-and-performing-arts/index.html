Degree Program

BA in Acting & Performing Arts

A practice-led undergraduate degree at IICS designed to shape performer–creators with the sensibility of both an actor and a director, ready for theatre, film, OTT, digital and immersive platforms.

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)

Academic Engagement:
4 Hours / Day | 5 Days / Week

Program Vision

Reflective & Ethical Artistic Foundation

The program develops thoughtful, ethically grounded performers who engage deeply with their craft, purpose, and responsibility as artists in society.

Holistic Performance & Storytelling Skills

Students gain a strong understanding of storytelling, staging, character psychology, spatial composition, and audience engagement across performance mediums.

Performer-Creator Development

Beyond acting, learners are trained to interpret text, shape performances, collaborate creatively with directors, and contribute meaningfully to the artistic process.

Leadership Across Contemporary Platforms

Graduates evolve into creative leaders capable of conceiving, directing, and leading original work for theatre, film, OTT, digital, and immersive platforms.

Curriculum Structure

A structured 6-semester journey from foundations to industry-ready expertise

Semester 1

Foundation of Acting & Performance

Introduction to Acting Techniques
Voice & Speech – I
Movement & Body Awareness – I
Theatre Appreciation & Dramatic Literature
Improvisation & Creative Expression
Performance Studio I

Semester 2

Acting, Writing & Stage Performance

Acting Methods & Scene Study
Voice & Speech – II
Movement & Physical Theatre
Writing for Performance
Indian & World Theatre Traditions
Performance Studio II – Scene Staging

Semester 3

Screen Acting, Script & Direction

Acting for Camera
Screenwriting Fundamentals
Direction & Blocking
Voice Acting & Dubbing
Audition Techniques
Performance Studio III – Actor–Director Lab

Semester 4

Direction, Production & Immersive Performance

Advanced Acting & Character Psychology
Direction for Theatre, Screen & XR
Script Development & Dramaturgy
Production Design & Stagecraft
Performance Ethics & Professional Practices
Performance Studio IV – Directed Production

Semester 5

Professional Practice & Apprenticeship

Paid Apprenticeship (Theatre / Film / OTT)
Professional Portfolio & Showreel Development
Industry Mentorship
Audition, Casting & Career Management

Semester 6

Final Production & Career Launch

Final Actor-Director Production
Writing, Adaptation & Original IP Development
Production Management & Budgeting
Media Law, Contracts & Artist Rights
Public Showcase, Festival & Jury Evaluation
